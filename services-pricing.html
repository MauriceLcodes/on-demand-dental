Oral Health Assessments & Screenings

Every appointment begins with a comprehensive oral health assessment to evaluate your overall oral and systemic health. Your assessment includes:

  • A thorough oral cancer screening
  • An evaluation of your oral hygiene and gum health
  • Blood pressure (hypertension) screening
  • A personalized cavity (caries) risk assessment
  • Intraoral photographs and digital X-rays when clinically indicated

These screenings help identify potential concerns early, allowing us to provide personalized recommendations and preventive care tailored to your unique oral health needs.

Included in every visit

Adult Prophylaxis (Routine Dental Cleaning)

Regular brushing and flossing are essential for maintaining oral health; however, they may not remove all plaque and tartar above the gumline. Dental prophylaxis is a preventive cleaning procedure designed to remove these deposits, reduce the risk of gum inflammation, and help maintain a healthy smile.

Prophylaxis is recommended for patients with healthy gums and is not appropriate for individuals with significant deposits below the gumline, active periodontal disease, or deeper periodontal pockets. In these cases, a different type of periodontal therapy may be recommended based on your specific needs.

For patients who qualify, routine prophylaxis appointments are typically recommended every six months to help maintain optimal oral health.

Scaling in the Presence of Gingivitis

Scaling in the presence of inflammation is a specialized dental cleaning designed to gently and effectively address gum inflammation caused by plaque and tartar buildup. This treatment requires additional time and attention compared to a routine cleaning due to the increased sensitivity and inflammation of the gums. Our goal is to help restore your gums to a healthier, more balanced state while providing a comfortable and personalized care experience.

$180

Scaling and Root Planing (Deep Cleaning)

Our scaling and root planing service is a specialized deep cleaning procedure designed to remove plaque, tartar, and bacterial buildup from above and below the gumline. This essential periodontal treatment helps reduce inflammation, support healthier gums, and create a cleaner environment for healing.

By addressing the underlying causes of gum disease, scaling and root planing can help restore gum health, improve your oral comfort, and support the long-term stability of your smile.

$150 Per quadrant

Periodontal Maintenance

Periodontal maintenance is a cleaning designed for patients with a history of periodontal disease who have completed treatment, such as scaling and root planing. Regular maintenance visits help control bacteria, reduce inflammation, and prevent disease progression.

To effectively manage periodontal health, maintenance appointments are typically recommended every three to four months based on your individual needs and risk factors.

$150

Fluoride Application

Topical fluoride is a treatment that is applied to teeth to help strengthen enamel and prevent cavities. It also helps with tooth sensitivity. Fluoride works by remineralizing enamel and inhibiting bacterial metabolism, which reduces the growth of plaque bacteria. This treatment is recommended for clients who are at high risk for cavities, tooth sensitivity, and recession.

Add-on $30

Oral Hygiene Instruction & Nutritional Counseling

Personalized oral hygiene education is provided based on each patient's individual needs, lifestyle, and oral health goals. For concierge clients receiving in-home care, guidance and support are provided to caregivers and family members to help maintain consistent oral care routines.

Nutritional counseling is available for patients with increased risk factors for tooth decay and enamel erosion. Through education and practical recommendations, patients are empowered to make informed choices that support healthier teeth, gums, and overall oral wellness.
