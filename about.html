About Gloria

Gloria Morejon, BSDH, RDHAP

Gloria founded On Demand Dental with a simple belief: quality dental hygiene care should be within everyone's reach, including patients who can't easily get to a dental office.

She holds a Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene (BSDH) from West Los Angeles College and has five years of professional experience. As a licensed Registered Dental Hygienist in Alternative Practice (RDHAP), she's completed the additional training and licensing required to provide dental hygiene care outside a traditional dental office, without a dentist on-site, which is what makes mobile visits possible.

She has cared for patients of every age and circumstance, from busy professionals to seniors in skilled nursing facilities. That experience shaped the practice's dual model: a calm, welcoming office in Woodland Hills, and mobile care that brings the same standard of treatment to homes and care facilities across the community.

Credentials

What is an RDHAP?

A Registered Dental Hygienist in Alternative Practice (RDHAP) is a licensed dental hygienist in California who has completed specialized training and licensing to provide dental hygiene services outside of a traditional dental office. RDHAPs can practice in settings like schools, nursing homes, hospitals, and patients' homes, working independently of a supervising dentist. It's the license that makes On Demand Dental's mobile visits possible.

Our Mission

Accessibility and compassion, in every visit

Too many people go without dental care, not because they don't value their health, but because mobility, anxiety, disability, or circumstance stand in the way. On Demand Dental exists to remove those barriers.

Every visit, in-office or mobile, is unhurried and judgment-free. We listen first, explain everything in plain language, and build care plans around each patient's real life.

Why Mobile Care Matters

Meeting patients where they are

For homebound seniors, residents of care facilities, and individuals with disabilities, a routine dental visit can be anything but routine. Untreated oral disease affects nutrition, comfort, confidence, and overall health.

Mobile hygiene care changes that. It brings preventive treatment directly to the people who need it most, on their schedule, in the place they feel safest.
