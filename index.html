Mobile Dental Care

We bring the care to you

For patients who can't easily visit an office, we deliver professional dental hygiene care at home, in assisted living, skilled nursing facilities, and other community settings.

Mobile care supports individuals with mobility limitations, physical or cognitive disabilities, anxiety, or other circumstances that make in-office visits difficult.
