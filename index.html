Concierge Dental Care

We bring the care to you

For patients who prefer not to visit an office, we bring concierge dental hygiene care directly to you, at home, in assisted living, in skilled nursing facilities, or wherever suits you best.

Our concierge service is built for patients who value convenience and comfort, along with those managing mobility limitations, physical or cognitive disabilities, anxiety, or other circumstances that make in-office visits difficult.
