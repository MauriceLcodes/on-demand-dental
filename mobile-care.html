Mobile Dental Care

Professional dental hygiene care, delivered wherever you are, because getting to an office shouldn't be a barrier to a healthy smile.

Who It's For

Care for those who can't come to us

Mobile dental care supports individuals with mobility or transportation limitations, physical or cognitive disabilities, dental anxiety or sensory sensitivities, chronic illness, or recovery from a medical procedure, along with anyone who finds long wait times and rigid scheduling hard to work around. It's also a practical option for busy caregivers coordinating care for a loved one.

Home Visits

Comfortable hygiene care in your own living room, no travel, no waiting rooms.

Assisted Living

Regular visits to residents, coordinated directly with families and facility staff.

Skilled Nursing Facilities

Bedside-capable care for patients with complex medical needs.

Accessibility Needs

Patient, adaptive care for physical, cognitive, or sensory challenges.

What to Expect

The same care, brought to you

We arrive with portable professional equipment and everything needed for a complete hygiene visit: a thorough cleaning, polishing, and a careful oral health check, all delivered at the patient's pace. All we ask is for a small, clear space, room enough for a chair and our equipment, so we can set up cleanly and efficiently.

Visits are unhurried. We take time to make patients comfortable, explain each step, and answer questions from family members or caregivers. After the visit, we share clear notes on what we found and any recommended follow-up.

Scheduling

How scheduling works

  1. Request a visit. Book online or call us. A family member or facility coordinator can request on the patient's behalf.
  2. We confirm the details. We'll discuss the patient's needs, location, and any medical considerations, and find a time that works.
  3. We come to you. On the day of your visit, we bring everything needed to provide your dental hygiene care. Appointment length varies based on your individual needs.

Ready to arrange a mobile visit?

We'll answer your questions and find a time that works for the patient and family.
