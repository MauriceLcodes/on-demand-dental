Our Services

Professional dental hygiene care, delivered in our Woodland Hills office or brought directly to you.

Dental Cleanings

A professional cleaning removes plaque and tartar that daily brushing and flossing can't reach, helping prevent cavities, gum disease, and bad breath. Every cleaning is thorough yet gentle, and paced around your comfort.

Whether it has been six months or several years since your last visit, you'll never be judged, just cared for. We'll leave your teeth feeling fresh and give you a clear picture of your oral health.

Teeth Whitening

A brighter smile can make a real difference in how you feel. Our professional whitening is safer and more effective than over-the-counter kits, with visible results and even, natural-looking coverage.

Every treatment is tailored to your sensitivity level and enamel health, so you leave with a noticeably whiter smile without the discomfort.

Oral Hygiene Instructions

Good habits between visits matter as much as the cleaning itself. We'll walk you through personalized brushing and flossing techniques, product recommendations, and simple adjustments that make a real difference in your gum health.

Whether you're managing sensitivity, working around dental work, or just want to brush smarter, you'll leave with clear, practical guidance suited to your mouth, not generic advice.

Referrals to Local Dentists

On Demand Dental focuses on hygiene and preventive care. If we spot something that needs a dentist's attention, like a cavity, a cracked tooth, or anything beyond hygiene scope, we'll let you know and, upon request, connect you with trusted local dentists.

No pressure, no upselling. Just an honest assessment and a referral when it's genuinely needed.

Care That Comes to You

Every service above can also be delivered outside the office, at home, in assisted living, or in skilled nursing facilities. We bring professional equipment and the same standard of care to patients who cannot easily travel.
