Privacy Policy

How we handle your information, in plain language.

Last updated: July 2026

What we collect

When you request an appointment through this website, we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email address, preferred visit type, and any notes you include. If you call or email us directly, we keep the contact information you share.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your request, schedule and confirm appointments, and communicate with you about your care. We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Please don't send medical details through the website

This contact form is not a secure medical channel. Please don't include health conditions, medications, or other medical details in the form. We'll discuss anything health-related privately by phone or in person.

How your information is stored

Form submissions are processed by Netlify, our website hosting provider, and delivered to our practice email. We keep submissions only as long as needed to manage scheduling and communication.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking for marketing purposes.

Your choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we have about you or to request that it be deleted. Reach us at ondemanddental@gmail.com or 818-821-5065.
