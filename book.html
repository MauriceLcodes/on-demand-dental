Frequently Asked Questions

How soon will I hear back after submitting a request?

We respond to appointment requests within one business day, usually much sooner. If it's urgent, call us at 818-821-5065.

Can I request a concierge visit for a family member?

Yes. Family members and facility coordinators regularly request visits on a patient's behalf. Just include the details in the notes field.

What areas do you serve for concierge visits?

We serve Woodland Hills and surrounding communities in the San Fernando Valley. Contact us if you're unsure whether your location is covered.

How long does a visit take?

Appointment length varies based on your individual needs. Whether you're visiting our Woodland Hills office or receiving concierge care, we'll provide a more accurate time estimate when we confirm your appointment.

Do you speak Spanish? (Se habla español)

Yes, we speak Spanish. You can call or submit your request in Spanish. — Sí, hablamos español. Puede llamar o enviar su solicitud en español.
